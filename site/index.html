a screen-time tracker for macOS and Windows

Know where your time goes.

Dopamine notes which app and window are in front, keeps that on your computer, and draws your day as a timeline, a breakdown by app and window, and a month of paint dabs. It pauses on its own when you lock the screen, sleep, or walk away.

    Honest numbers, no effort

    • Zero effort

      Nothing to start, stop or label. Time stops counting when you lock the screen, sleep, or leave the keyboard alone for five minutes.

    • Glances don't count

      Windows in front for under five seconds are left out, a crashed session can't inflate a day, and today is compared with yesterday only up to the same time.

    • Categories that fit you

      Work, study, social, entertainment and other are detected for you. Click a window to change it, for that one title or for every title containing some words, like a course code or a channel name.

    • Forget, or pause

      Erase a window or a session and its title is overwritten on disk. Pause for 15 minutes, an hour, until tomorrow, or until you resume, to keep something from being recorded at all.

    • Hide any app

      Leave an app out of the numbers entirely. Dopamine's own windows are hidden from the start.

    • Light on your machine

      A native menu bar or tray agent, one SQLite file, and a dashboard with no charting libraries that holds 60 fps. In English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.

    How a category is decided

    Dopamine looks at the evidence in this order and stops at the first answer.

    1. Your own choice for the appDiscord counts as Study because you said so
    2. The site, for browsersTwo Sum - LeetCode - Google Chrome → Study
    3. The app, by nameidea64, LeagueClientUx, WXWork
    4. Apps others agreed onOnly if you opted in, and only for apps the rules don't know
    5. What the app says about itselfThe macOS App Store category; on Windows, the publisher and install path (steamapps means a game)
    6. The window titlejavaw showing “Minecraft”

    The rules live in one file shared by the dashboard and the macOS agent, and are checked against 115 real process names and tab titles. Categories are estimates: correct them in the dashboard when they're wrong.

    Your activity never leaves your computer

    Window titles, times and usage stay in a local SQLite database, served only to localhost behind a pairing code. There is no account to create.

    A daily check for new versions

    Sparkle on macOS and Velopack on Windows download updates from GitHub Releases and install them when the app quits. Choose Update and restart to apply a ready update. No activity records are uploaded. Turn off future checks and downloads from the dashboard footer.

    Start tracking

    Click the menu bar or tray icon to open the dashboard in its own window. Closing the window leaves tracking running.

    macOS 12 or later

    1. Open Dopamine-mac.dmg and drag Dopamine onto Applications.
    2. Open it. This early build isn't notarised, so macOS asks first: choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    3. Allow Accessibility access when asked, so Dopamine can read window titles. Without it, only app names are recorded.
    4. Click the hourglass in the menu bar, then Open Dashboard. Tick Open at login there to start with your Mac.
    Download for macOS

    Windows 10 or later

    1. Unzip Dopamine-win.zip anywhere and run DopamineWin.exe. No .NET installation is needed.
    2. Right-click the tray icon (or double-click it) and choose Open Dashboard.
